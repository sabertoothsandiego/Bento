Uses

This berry topping is best served warm over ice-cream, pancakes(especially pancakes) waffles, crepes, french toast or in a croissant with whipped cream!(did i mention pancakes?)

Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 cups Berries of choice(i recommend mixed, but any berry will work frozen and fresh)
    2.5 cups Sugar
    2 tbsp Lemon Juice
    1 tbsp Cornstarch

    • Instructions

    1. In a small bowl, mix your cornstarch with an equal amount of water. mix until there are no lumps and set aside for later.
    2. Put berries in a small pot over medium-high heat until mushy(about four minutes for fresh and about six minutes for frozen).
    3. Turn the heat down to medium and add all your sugar, mixing slowly until fully dissolved.
    4. Once all your sugar is mixed in, add your lemon juice and mix until fully incorporated.
    5. Turn the heat down to Medium-Low and add your cornstarch slurry. Let it simmer for 3 minutes, mixing every minute.
    6. Adjust sugar and lemon to taste if necessary, then turn off the heat and let it rest for at least two minutes before serving fresh, or store in the fridge for up to a week.
    7. ENJOY!
